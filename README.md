ROASTERA Coffee Co.

Better Coffee. Brighter Days.

ROASTERA is a premium coffee gear storefront created for coffee lovers, home baristas, and anyone who wants to build a better brewing ritual at home.

The brand focuses on carefully selected coffee equipment, brewing tools, drinkware, and barista essentials with a clean, warm, editorial-inspired visual identity.

☕ About ROASTERA

ROASTERA Coffee Co. brings together the tools needed to create a better coffee experience at home.

From espresso machines and precision grinders to pour-over brewers, kettles, scales, cups, storage solutions, and barista accessories, the store is designed around one idea:

Great coffee starts with the right tools.

The website combines premium product presentation with an easy shopping experience, responsive layouts, product discovery, and useful brewing-focused content.

✨ Brand Vision

ROASTERA is designed to feel like a modern specialty coffee brand rather than a generic online store.

📸 Photography Direction

Product photography should remain clean, realistic, and professionally presented.

Each product should:

Be clearly visible

Be correctly framed

Have its own dedicated image

Avoid unnecessary UI elements

Avoid fake pricing embedded into the image

Avoid buttons or badges inside photographs

Maintain consistent visual quality

Work naturally inside square product containers

Lifestyle imagery can be used for hero sections and editorial content, while product cards should prioritize clear product presentation.

🔐 Privacy & Data

The frontend does not require a backend database for the core demo shopping experience.

Cart and wishlist information can be stored locally in the user's browser using localStorage.

No sensitive payment information should be stored directly in the frontend.

For a production store, payment processing and customer accounts should be handled through a secure payment provider and backend service.
